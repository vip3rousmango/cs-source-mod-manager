# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, welcoming, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- being respectful and constructive;
- accepting responsibility for mistakes and learning from them;
- focusing feedback on the work, not the person;
- respecting different experiences, backgrounds, and opinions; and
- keeping discussions and contributions relevant to the project scope.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, sexualized language or imagery, doxxing, spam, and deliberately disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Do not submit malicious code, undisclosed vulnerable dependencies, unrelated bulk changes, or automated pull requests that do not solve a documented project problem.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers through the contact method in `SECURITY.md` or the repository's private contact options. Do not create a public issue for a safety concern. Maintainers may edit, hide, close, or reject content and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its contributors.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces, issue trackers, pull requests, chats, and when someone is officially representing the project.
